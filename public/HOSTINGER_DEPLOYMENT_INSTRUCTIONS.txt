HOSTINGER UPLOAD GUIDE - KARAM UTSAV WEBSITE
=============================================

NO GITHUB ACCOUNT NEEDED!
Follow these 4 simple steps to make your website live on Hostinger in 2 minutes:

STEP 1:
Log in to your Hostinger hPanel (https://hpanel.hostinger.com)

STEP 2:
Go to Websites -> Manage -> File Manager -> open folder 'public_html'

STEP 3:
Upload 'karamutsav-hostinger-build.zip' into 'public_html'

STEP 4:
Right-click on the uploaded zip file and choose 'Extract'.
Extract directly into 'public_html'.

Done! Visit your website in your browser: https://yourdomain.com

WHY DID GITHUB UPLOAD FAIL BEFORE?
Hostinger shared hosting runs Apache/LiteSpeed web servers. When you upload
raw source files from GitHub, Hostinger doesn't compile React/TypeScript
files. The 'karamutsav-hostinger-build.zip' contains the fully compiled
production HTML, CSS, JavaScript, and .htaccess ready to serve instantly.
